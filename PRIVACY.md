# Oddfish Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Oddfish is designed to work without an account and without collecting personal
data.

## Data collection

Oddfish does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does
not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party analytics
SDKs.

## Data stored on your device

Game history, settings, personal bests, and purchase-entitlement state are kept
on your device in the app's own storage. Oddfish does not send this information
to the developer or to any external server.

You can remove locally stored Oddfish data by deleting the app from your device.

## In-app purchases

Oddfish offers an optional non-consumable in-app purchase. Purchases and
restoration are processed by Apple through the App Store. Oddfish receives only
the signed transaction and entitlement information needed to unlock purchased
features; it does not receive or store your payment-card details. Apple's own
privacy policy governs information processed by Apple.

## Children

Because Oddfish does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect
personal data from children.

## Changes

If Oddfish's data practices change, this policy will be updated before a version
with those changes is released.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the public Oddfish source repository:
<https://github.com/johnsquared7/chess-fun/issues>.
